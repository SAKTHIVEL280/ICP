About the Portal

The Internal Complaint Portal enables employees to quickly log issues related to IT support, Facilities management, HR, Maintenance, and Workplace Safety. Once submitted, your issue is routed automatically to the corresponding department. A technician is assigned, and you are notified at every step. You must verify and close the ticket once the technician marks it as Resolved.
